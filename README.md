# BCG Strategy Consulting Case Study

**Educational simulation of a full BCG-style strategy engagement for fictional company BeyondTech.**  
All data, financials, survey responses, and stakeholder profiles are synthetic or provided by the exercise. This is a technical demonstration for portfolio purposes — not a real client project, production system, or live recommendation.

## Executive Summary & Conclusion

**Recommendation**: Proceed with a 0% APR 18-24 month installment financing program focused on flagship devices, targeted at 25-44 year olds with $50k+ household income.

**Why it works**:
- Strong market demand (77% of 450 surveyed consumers have used similar plans; 74% say it increases likelihood of buying premium tech).
- Proven competitor results (Company Y with mature 0% plans: +6-9% sales/profit growth; Company Z: short-term profit dip during rollout, then +18% net income rebound and 2%→8% market share gain).
- Internal alignment is high (Sales, CFO, CMO see clear revenue/brand upside; Tech & Ops concerns are manageable with proper planning).
- Risks (bad debt, brand perception, cash flow delay) have clear mitigations (strict credit scoring, transparent 0% marketing, dynamic provisioning, no-penalty early payoff).

**12-month phased roadmap**:
1. 0-3 months: Pilot on 2-3 flagships in NA/EU, integrate payment platform, train teams, soft launch to high-intent segments.
2. 3-6 months: Full launch with A/B testing on terms, monitor KPIs (conversion, bad debt <2%, NPS), iterate.
3. 6-12 months: Global expansion, loyalty tie-in, personalized offers, target 20%+ of sales via financing.

This case study demonstrates end-to-end strategic thinking: from market research and financial benchmarking to customer survey design, stakeholder mapping, and actionable recommendations. All analysis stays within the provided (simulated) data.

## Task Results Summary

### Task 1: Research & Messaging
**Input**: Pyramid Principle guide, consumer trends article, TechCo case study, sample email.  
**Output**: Structured viability report + professional email draft.  
**Key finding**: Installment financing meets evolving consumer needs for affordability (81% prioritize monthly cost). Competitors like TechCo gained 2%→8% market share with transparent 0% plans.

### Task 2: Competitor Financial Benchmarking
**Input**: 3-year financials for Company X (no financing), Y (5+ years 0% 12mo), Z (introduced Y0-Y1, 2% interest up to 24mo).  
**Output**: Impact analysis slide with YoY deltas and visual ratings.  
**Key finding**:

| Metric          | X (baseline) | Y (mature)     | Z (new launch)      |
|-----------------|--------------|----------------|---------------------|
| Sales Y0-Y1    | +3.8%       | +6.1%         | +13.0%             |
| Net Income Y0-Y1 | +4.2%      | +9.1%         | -13.7% (setup cost) |
| Net Income Y1-Y2 | +3.1%      | +8.7%         | +18.1% (rebound)   |
| Interest Revenue | 0          | 0             | +148% (2% plans)   |

**Conclusion**: Short-term pain for new adopters, strong long-term upside. 0% plans drive volume without heavy interest revenue reliance.

### Task 3: Survey Design
**Input**: Best-practice tips, sample remote-work survey.  
**Output**: 13-question customer survey (demographics, usage, preferences, trade-offs, concerns).  
**Key feature**: Explicit A/B/C trade-off question on $1200 laptop (0% 24mo vs 0% 12mo + down payment vs 3.9% 18mo) to reveal true priorities.

### Task 4: Survey Analysis
**Input**: 450-response dataset (demographics, usage by age/income, motivators, concerns, term/rate matrix, word cloud).  
**Output**: Synthesized insights (used in summary slide).  
**Key finding**:

| Insight                  | Data Point                  | Implication                  |
|--------------------------|-----------------------------|------------------------------|
| Prior usage             | 77%                        | High familiarity            |
| Purchase lift           | 74% more likely            | Strong conversion driver    |
| Top concern             | Hidden fees (101 responses)| Need total-cost transparency|
| Preferred term          | Longer at 0%               | 18-24mo core offer          |

### Task 5: Stakeholder Mapping
**Input**: 5 executive bios (Head of Sales, CFO, CTO, CMO, COO).  
**Output**: Completed influence map (PDF version).  
**Key finding**:

| Stakeholder | Priority | Main Motivator                  | Main Concern                     |
|-------------|----------|---------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Daniel (Sales) | High    | Revenue & premium deal volume  | Training time & sales cycles    |
| Elie (CFO)     | High    | New revenue stream & ASP lift  | Bad debt / AR ballooning        |
| Raj (CTO)      | Medium  | Platform modernization         | Integration & PCI compliance    |
| Michael (CMO)  | High    | Brand innovation & loyalty     | Customer confusion / fees       |
| Asha (COO)     | Medium  | Demand predictability          | Service volume & supply scaling |

### Task 6: Executive One-Pager
**Input**: All prior outputs + summary template.  
**Output**: One-page recommendation slide with roadmap.  
**Key feature**: Concise, action-oriented, data-backed for busy executives.
